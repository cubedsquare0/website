How to Play Cubed Square

Cubed Square is a sandbox game where you can build, explore, and survive in a blocky world.

Game Modes

  • Survival: Gather resources, build shelters, and survive the night.
  • Creative: Unlimited resources, fly, and build freely.
  • Adventure: Explore custom maps and stories.
